Module 1: S!aP Connect (Field, CCTV, Gateway Connectivity) 
S!aP Connect is the vendor-neutral integration layer. At Al Dhaher it connects to each of the 
42 bay RTUs over Modbus/OPC -UA, to the IP cameras over RTSP, to the gate barriers and 
network-flow instruments, and to the K -net/MEW payment gateway over HTTPS. A  local 
Edge at the Local Control Centre buffers data and transmits it to the Salmiya data centre over 
the secured WAN. Connect is northbound and read -only towards the field — it consumes 
values and does not write to, or alter, the RTUs, valves or control l oops. Store-and-forward 
buffering ensures no data loss during any WAN interruption. 
Module 2: S!aP Datalake (The Central Historian) 
The Datalake is the central historian the tender requires. It stores every ingested transaction 
and signal from all 42 bays plus surveillance, gate and network -flow events in an open, high-
performance time -series database with 10 -year+ retention (configura ble and extensible), 
organised by site, bay and device. Built on open technology, it avoids proprietary lock -in and 
is continuously replicated to the DR site at South Surra for business continuity.

5.5 Data Storage Sizing (10-Year Historian) & Growth 
Storage depends on the number of tags/streams per bay, the effective logging rate after 
change-based compression, transaction volume, and video retention. With 42 bays plus 
surveillance metadata, network-flow and payment records, a practical planning estimate is on 
the order of a few TB per year of compressed time -series and transactional data (excluding 
raw video, which is retained 60 days on the NVR tier). Astrikos recommends provisioning 16–
32 TB usable at Salmiya (and matched capacity at DR) to cover 10 -year retention plus 
headroom and indexing. The open historian scales horizontally, and retention, roll -up and 
archival policies are configurable — so storage grows predictably with bays, transactions and 
retention. Exact figures are confirmed once samplin g rates and retention per stream are 
agreed during design.

app and APIs; a DMZ/Extractor zone that sanitises incoming data; and an Ope rations zone 
housing the Core and Datalake, isolated from internet -facing networks. The Edge sits at the
